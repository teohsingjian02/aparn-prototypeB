Publish with APARN

Contributions from APARN events can be published in APARN’s channel of the Journal for Artistic Research (JAR), SAR’s open-access journal. Channel pieces are editorially reviewed, not peer reviewed. Papers can also be reworked and submitted to JAR’s peer-reviewed issues through its open calls.

Proposed A proposal for review

APARN Annual Publication

A year-end collection of members’ research in progress. The proposal: participating members’ research-progress contributions are compiled into one publication by the end of each publication year. It uses the same contribution you prepare for your update at the SAR Conference, so there is nothing extra to write.

Template for Annual Publication

Prepare one research update for your SAR presentation and, if you choose, APARN’s year-end publication.

  1. 1Opt inTick the publication option when you send your contribution. It is optional and unchecked by default.
  2. 2Share your updatePresent the same contribution at the APARN Gathering at the SAR Conference, in 5–10 minutes.
  3. 3Editorial reviewEditors may contact you about revisions, formatting and final approval.
  4. 4Revise the same contributionCorrections and new progress update your existing contribution. You do not start a new submission.
  5. 5The year-end collectionApproved contributions are collected by the end of the publication year.

Good to know

  • Opting in does not guarantee inclusion.
  • Opting in does not transfer your copyright.
  • Permission to reproduce images, to record your presentation or to show contact details is asked for separately.

The idea grows from the “yearbook” pilot that SAR’s APARN page describes for 2025, documenting artistic research projects from across the region.

Still to decide: Editors and editorial process; Publisher and format (print, PDF or Research Catalogue); Schedule and deadlines; Licence and copyright terms; Whether an ISBN or DOI is used.

Where APARN research is published now

Journal channel

APARN in the Journal for Artistic Research

APARN’s channel in JAR, SAR’s open-access journal, holds contributions from APARN conferences and highlights JAR publications from the region. Channel pieces are editorially reviewed, not peer reviewed. Proposals for the channel go to the Director, and calls are shared through SAR Announcements.

  • Edited by Danny Butt, Kurniawan Adi Saputro and Gunalan Nadarajan
  • 28 pieces as of November 2026
