APARN Gathering at the SAR Conference 2027

Details to be confirmed SAR 2027: 21–23 April 2027 / Bratislava, Slovakia

Gather with fellow members at SAR 2027 in Bratislava to share where your research is now, in a short update of 5–10 minutes. The APARN team will confirm the date, time and format. SAR’s own call and registration are separate, on sar2027.eu.

Label
APARN Gathering at SAR 2027
Host
SAR’s 18th International Conference on Artistic Research, “Beyond the Familiar”, at the Academy of Performing Arts in Bratislava (VŠMU)
Format
Proposed format: short research updates of 5–10 minutes. Date, time and whether it is online, in person or both: to be confirmed.
