APARN Conference 2022 Planet, ecology and climate Online

Archive / APARN Conference

Artistic research for the planet

Held 25 November 2022 / Online

A day of research on local ecologies, extraction and our responsibilities to the planet, starting from Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak’s “Imperative to Reimagine the Planet”. Presenters came from Thailand, Aotearoa New Zealand, Australia, Indonesia, the Philippines, Austria and beyond.

Label
#APARN2022
Host
Online gathering convened by Kurniawan Adi Saputro, Gunalan Nadarajan and Danny Butt
Format
15 short presentations, ending with the inaugural meeting of APARN as a SAR Special Interest Group
Practice
Planet, ecology and climate
Note
10:00–15:00 Yogyakarta, 14:00–19:00 Melbourne

Highlights

  • Projects from Chiang Rai to Maui: performance remapping, glacial breath, waste research, tropical temporality and mapping extinctions
  • Ended with the inaugural meeting of APARN as a Special Interest Group of the Society for Artistic Research

Event organisers

  • Kurniawan Adi SaputroConvenor
  • Gunalan NadarajanConvenor
  • Danny ButtConvenor

Listed for this edition. The network’s convenors are on the About page.

Programme

  1. 10:15Chiengsaen-Saensanuk: A Performance Approach to the Artistic Remapping of Chiangrai’s Cultural Ecosystems in Northern Thailand. Pornrat Damrhung and Lowell Skar
  2. 10:30Haupapa: The Chilled Breath of Rakamaomao. Ron Bull, Janine Randerson, Rachael Shearer, Heather Purdie, Stefan Marks
  3. 10:45The 3R Dance Project. Joanna Cook and Janaína Moraes
  4. 11:00Tender Territory and Micro Reality. Hara Shin
  5. 11:15Creative (Mis)Understandings: Methodologies of Inspiration. Johannes Kretz and Zheng Kuo / Si Pehbowen
  6. 11:30Defining ‘Well-Played’ through Inclusive Board Games for the Blind. Nurul Aqmarina Ardani (Arin)
  7. 12:15The Spoil Grounds: fiction and writing in the excesses of extraction. Therese Keogh
  8. 12:30Winds~Waves~Wings Festival Maui. Florian Dombois, Andy Graydon and Helene Romakin
  9. 12:45Waste Research. Dyah Retno
  10. 13:00Virtual Aesthetic: a Virtual Tour of the National Museum during the Covid-19 Pandemic. Ricky Widyananda Putra
  11. 13:45Dreaming the future through tropical temporality. Erika M. Carreon
  12. 14:00When Our Feet Never Touch the Ground. Joan Marie Kelly
  13. 14:15Terrania. Nicky Stott and Nat Grant
  14. 14:30Mapping Extinctions. Linda Knight
