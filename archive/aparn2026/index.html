APARN Conference 2026 Voice and authorship Singapore

Archive / APARN Conference

Who Speaks? Authorship, Authority, and Authenticity

Held 27–29 July 2026 / Singapore

APARN joined NAFA’s practice-research exchange to ask who speaks through an artwork: questions of voice, authorship and authenticity in collaborative, embodied, traditional and AI-assisted practice.

Label
ADPRex × APARN 2026
Host
Nanyang Academy of Fine Arts (NAFA), University of the Arts Singapore, with the Arts & Design Practice Research Exchange (ADPRex)
Format
In person, in the NAFA Studio Theatre and Lee Foundation Theatre: papers, performances, creative interventions and video essays of up to 20 minutes. Free to attend.
Practice
Voice and authorship

Highlights

  • Keynotes: Celine Parreñas Shimizu (UCLA School of Theater, Film and Television) and farid rakun (ruangrupa)
  • Performance: Handle with Care by Ontroerend Goed, arranged with Esplanade – Theatres on the Bay
  • 14 contributions already published in APARN’s JAR channel

Event organisers

  • ADPRex team, NAFAHost and organiseradprex@nafa.edu.sg

Listed for this edition. The network’s convenors are on the About page.

Read the original call (archived, now closed)

Encountering an artwork, the question of ‘who speaks’ and whose perspective and viewpoint we are receiving underscores our interaction with what we see, hear, and feel about a piece. … We invite presenters to consider how authorship is shaped and articulated in their work using new (and old) technologies.
