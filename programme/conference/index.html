APARN Conference

Each year a different host opens its doors to artists, researchers, educators and communities from across the Asia Pacific, to share practices, test ideas and build friendships that last.

APARN Conference 2027 RMIT University Vietnam Vietnam

APARN2027 / RMIT University Vietnam

The next APARN conference will be hosted by RMIT University Vietnam: APARN’s first meeting in Vietnam. Dates, city, theme and the call for contributions will be announced through the APARN Google Group.

What to expect

  • Papers, performances, creative interventions, workshops and video essays, usually 15–20 minutes each.
  • Open to artistic researchers in the region and to those elsewhere with an interest in it.
  • Publication in APARN’s channel of the Journal for Artistic Research; some hosts add partner journals.
  • APARN2026 was free to attend. Travel bursaries were not available.
