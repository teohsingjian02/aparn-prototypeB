APARN Gathering at SAR 2027

SAR’s 18th International Conference on Artistic Research, “Beyond the Familiar”, runs 21–23 April 2027 at the Academy of Performing Arts in Bratislava. Join fellow members to share where your research is now.

Details to be confirmed The APARN team will confirm the date, time and format.

SAR 2027 has its own call (closing 25 November 2026) and registration on sar2027.eu↗ (opens in a new tab). The APARN gathering is separate.

5–10 minutes. Your way.

The proposed format gives each participant about 5–10 minutes to share current research and progress. Work in progress is welcome, and so are visual, performative and sound formats.

From contribution to a 5–10-minute update

Your talk is a short, lively version of the same contribution. Visual, performative and sound formats are welcome within the time.

Suggested timing for a 5- or 10-minute update
PartFrom your contribution5-minute update10-minute update
1 Question and contextSections 1–3about 1 minuteabout 2 minutes
