APARN Research Progress Contribution

One contribution for your SAR presentation and, if you choose, APARN’s year-end publication.

Use this template to describe where your research is now. Work in progress is welcome: you do not need finished results. Replace the grey guidance text with your own words and keep the eight headings.

Aim for about two pages: 400–600 words and one or two images (draft limits, still under review). Please leave out email addresses: your contact details go in the submission form.

  1. 1 Project or research title

    Up to 20 words A clear working title. A subtitle is welcome.

    Working title: subtitle

  2. 2 Authors and context

    Each author’s name; affiliation, or “independent practitioner”; and the country or region where the research takes place. Please leave out email addresses: contact details are collected separately and are not published.

    • Author name(s):
    • Affiliation or independent-practitioner status:
    • Country or region of research activity:

  3. 3 Research question, purpose or creative inquiry

    70–110 words What are you asking, exploring or making, and why does it matter to you and to others?

    Write about your question or inquiry here.

  4. 4 Artistic practice, methods or approach

    90–130 words How you work: media, materials, sites, collaborators and methods. Use the language of your own discipline.

    Describe your practice and approach here.

  5. 5 Current progress and emerging insights

    140–200 words Where the work is now: what has happened, what you are noticing, and what is still in development.

    Share your progress and what is emerging here.

  6. 6 Next steps, questions and invitations

    70–110 words What comes next, what you would like feedback on, and any collaboration you are looking for.

    Note your next steps and questions here.

  7. 7 Images or documentation

    One or two images, or stills from video, sound or performance work. For each, give a caption, a credit, and confirm you have permission to share it.

    • Image 1: caption, credit, permission confirmed (yes/no):
    • Image 2 (optional): caption, credit, permission confirmed (yes/no):

  8. 8 References and links

    Up to five references or links, in any citation style used consistently.

    Reference or link

How to complete it

  1. 1Download the DOCX. It also opens in Google Docs, Pages and LibreOffice.
  2. 2Replace each piece of grey guidance with your own text. Keep the eight headings.
  3. 3Aim for about two pages: 400–600 words plus one or two images.
  4. 4Add a caption, credit and permission note for every image.
  5. 5Save as DOCX or PDF, named Surname_ShortTitle.docx (or .pdf).
  6. 6Use the same file to prepare your 5–10-minute update, and attach it in the submission form.

Presentation guide

From contribution to a 5–10-minute update

Your talk is a short, lively version of the same contribution. Visual, performative and sound formats are welcome within the time.

Suggested timing for a 5- or 10-minute update
PartFrom your contribution5-minute update10-minute update
1 Question and contextSections 1–3about 1 minuteabout 2 minutes
2 Practice and approachSection 4about 1½ minutesabout 3 minutes
3 Progress and insightsSections 5 and 7about 1½ minutesabout 3 minutes
4 Next stepsSection 6about 1 minuteabout 2 minutes
